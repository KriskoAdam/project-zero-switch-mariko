# Source map and publication exclusions

This sanitized tree was prepared from the local `switch-research` workspace. Historical reports from `switch-research/reports/` are in `reports/`; locally kept Phase 5 and Phase 12–14 lab reports are in `reports/project-zero-local/`. The original local tree remains unchanged.

Custom host scripts and protocol probes are under `tools/`; synthetic models, campaigns and results are under `lab/`. The released Switch application at `PROJECT_ZERO/apps/PROJECT_ZERO/` is intentionally absent.

The local Ghidra installation (third-party software distribution), virtual environments, caches, binary Nintendo artifacts, keys, application build products, and the reconstructed devkitPro/newlib object/disassembly were not copied. The permissively licensed libnx source excerpts are retained with their notice. The newlib object hash and the reason the object is omitted remain documented in the historical reports.

USB serial fields, private network addresses, local account names, and private absolute home paths were redacted in the publication copy. Raw evidence here means retained protocol/application logs and descriptors after those privacy redactions; no copyrighted firmware or console dump is included.

The local archive has no standalone Phase 1 or Phase 2 report files. Phase history below distinguishes archived material from missing phase-specific artifacts rather than reconstructing absent reports.
