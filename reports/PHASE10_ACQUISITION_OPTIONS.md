# PROJECT ZERO — Phase 10: eligible acquisition options

## Options that meet the research gate

1. **Published by Nintendo or rights holder.** Only use executables/metadata that the publisher makes available for analysis, or explicitly allows in a security or research program. Require the original hash, build identity and allowed analysis scope.
2. **Artifact from an authorized researcher or laboratory.** Accept only an unencrypted, unaltered and shareable artifact for which the provider confirms the right to provide and analyze it. Request origin, build ID, hash, format, architecture, associated permission/IPC metadata and any redistribution restrictions.
3. **Publicly published unencrypted artifact under a clear license.** Check the license and signature/hash of the publisher. If only the source code is public, classify it as SDK/Project source; not present it as a compiled HOS.
4. **An artifact that the researcher already legitimately owns and is allowed to analyze.** He can provide it to the work tree only if its conditions allow this analysis and sharing. Record the origin and verify the hash against a trusted manifest or publisher before processing.

In the absence of a signed manifest, SHA-256 provides byte identity, **not proof of authenticity or authority**. In such a case, require independent confirmation from the originator; two unverified mirrors do not substitute for an authoritative origin.

## Which is not considered an approved path in this work

- Downloading firmware from unofficial mirrors or obscure repositories.
- Extracting content from the console, obtaining or using console keys, bypassing encryption, boot protection or manipulating partitions.
- Use of privately leaked files without proven authorization to possess, analyze and share them.
- The assumption that purchasing a console or update automatically entitles you to obtain/redistribute its binaries. Legal authorization depends on origin, license and jurisdiction; the submission of the artefact must be accompanied by confirmation of these rights.

No acquisition or extraction was performed. The physical console remains out of testing.
