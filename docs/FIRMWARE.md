# Firmware target

The primary research target is Horizon OS **23.0.1**, as reported by the console owner and recorded during the physical-device workflow. The project does not have an authenticated firmware image, system-module binary, NPDM, or console dump for that version. The exact internal implementation of HOS 23.0.1 has therefore not been analyzed.

Firmware compatibility claims must identify their source and exact version. Do not infer that an exploit works on Mariko 23.0.1 merely because it works on another Switch revision, an older firmware, a library, or an application. The goal is to find a software-only entry point for the specified target; accurate version information is essential to that work.
